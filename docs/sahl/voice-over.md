# English Voice-Over Script — Sahl tour (synchronized to implemented app)

All actions below are performed by the app. Times are tour-relative.

| # | Time | Screen | Action | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | Login | Enter username + password, press Login | "Welcome to the stock and shop manager. Sign in with your user account to open the main dashboard." |
| 2 | 0:20–0:50 | Dashboard | Green tiles: Reports, Invoices, Treasury, Accounts, Goods; brand shows YOUR company name | "The black desktop dashboard. Five green tiles open reports, invoices, treasury, accounts and goods. The brand tile shows your own company name from settings." |
| 3 | 0:50–1:10 | Dashboard | Blue row: account/product management, program settings, guided tour | "The blue row manages accounts and products, program settings, and a guided tour." |
| 4 | 1:10–1:30 | Tour overlay | Click tour tile, step Back/Next, finish | "The tour explains each section, then jumps straight to a new sales invoice." |
| 5 | 1:30–2:10 | Products | Search item, open green form, edit price, post opening stock to warehouse | "Products: search by name or barcode, edit in the green form, and post opening stock as an auditable movement." |
| 6 | 2:10–2:40 | Accounts | New customer with phone, VAT, opening balance; open statement | "Customers and suppliers with tax IDs and opening balances, plus a live statement of invoices and payments." |
| 7 | 2:40–3:40 | Sales | Search item, add lines, pick price list, F5 save, F6 print | "The sales workspace: find items by code or barcode, choose one of three price lists, save with F5, print with F6." |
| 8 | 3:40–4:00 | Purchase | Supplier + warehouse, save PO+GRN, stock increases | "Purchases create an order and a goods receipt together, posting stock at once." |
| 9 | 4:00–4:20 | Returns | Sales return linked to original invoice; purchase return subtracts stock | "Returns always reference the original document — never silent edits." |
| 10 | 4:20–4:50 | Cash | Receipt voucher, expense with category, ledger balance | "Receipts and expenses update the treasury ledger immediately, with balance guards." |
| 11 | 4:50–5:20 | Vault | Stocktake count, atomic transfer, cheque register, installment collection | "Count sessions, validated warehouse transfers, cheques and installment follow-up." |
| 12 | 5:20–5:40 | Reports | Daily movement, sales analysis, valuation; print | "Every report comes from posted records and prints cleanly." |
